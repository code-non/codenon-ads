21Ribbon

The logo is a ribbon, so here it is drawn as a single unbroken line. It enters the front from the left, draws the "un" with the N's diagonal woven through it, and runs off the far edge. On the back it re-enters at the same height and turns up past the QR, so the line goes all the way round the card. Null is a vermilion line on paper; Void is sky on black. The real mark stays small beside the wordmark.
