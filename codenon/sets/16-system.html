16System

A full corporate identity system, the kind that runs across cards, letterhead and screens. A brand-colour panel holds the mark; the open field holds the person, set on a strict grid with ruled contact lines. Null's panel is sun yellow with a navy mark; Void's is cobalt with a white mark on a navy field. On the back the panel moves to the other side to frame the QR.
