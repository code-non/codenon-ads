03Lumen

Futuristic minimalism made with light alone. On Null's card a slit of warm apricot light cuts a black card in two. On Void's the light bends around a point it can't escape, with an ultraviolet ring and a thin line of light passing behind it. On the back the QR sits exactly in the beam, so the light lands on it.
